# Voltaic Pylon

The **Voltaic Pylon** is an upside-down tower structure found in the ceiling of the [Voltaic Heaps](https://stardust-labs-mc.github.io/incendium-wiki/biomes/voltaic-heap/) biome, consisting of several combat rooms stacked vertically, with one central platforming room and two adjacent smaller towers. The structure holds the [Voltaic Trident](https://stardust-labs-mc.github.io/incendium-wiki/items/voltaic-trident/) at its top floor above the bedrock roof, guarded by Breezes and Skeletons.
