# Forbidden Castle

The **Forbidden Castle** is a large, randomly generated jigsaw structure, found in the [Ash Barrens](https://stardust-labs-mc.github.io/incendium-wiki/biomes/ash-barrens) biome inhabited by highly-skilled, specialized Piglin Brutes. It is composed of dozens of blackstone towers with procedurally generated rooms, with the structure perimeter surrounded by walls. It holds high value treasure with a total of 10 powerful tools that can be found, guarded by 7 unique Piglins classes. It is regarded as the most challenging but rewarding Incendium structure.
