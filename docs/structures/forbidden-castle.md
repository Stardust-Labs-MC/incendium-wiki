### Forbidden Castle

The **Forbidden Castle** is a large, randomly generated jigsaw structure, found in the [Ash Barrens](https://stardust-labs-mc.github.io/incendium-wiki/biomes/ash-barrens) biome inhabited by highly-skilled, specialized Piglin Brutes. It is composed of dozens of blackstone towers with procedurally generated rooms, with the structure perimeter surrounded by walls. It contains high-value treasure, with 10 new powerful tools that can be found, guarded by 7 unique Piglin classes. It is considered the most challenging yet rewarding Incendium structure.
