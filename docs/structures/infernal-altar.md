### Infernal Altar

The **Infernal Altar** is a dome-shaped structure found in the [Infernal Dunes](https://stardust-labs-mc.github.io/incendium-wiki/biomes/infernal-dunes/) biome, used as an arena to summon and fight the [Hovering Inferno](https://stardust-labs-mc.github.io/incendium-wiki/bosses/hovering-inferno/). It consists of a central interior with a summoning altar, and a basement with loot and vaults unlocked from defeating the Hovering Inferno.
