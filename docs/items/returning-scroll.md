## Description

The Scroll of returning is an item that can be used in the Nether with the ability to send you back to the Overworld at the location you entered the Nether from.

When activated the item will float in the air and create a small ring underneath itself, after 6 seconds every player and most passive/friendly mobs in the 3 block radius ring will be warped to the location the user of the Scroll entered the Nether from.

Before the warp begins the Scroll will either break or have a 60% chance to be preserved and drop allowing to be used again.


## Obtaining

The Scroll of Returning is dropped by [Illusionists](../mobs/sanctum-illusionist.md) that spawn inside the [Sanctum](../structures/sanctum.md).


## History

The Scroll of Returning is a Patron item requested by Redhawk

## Modifications
This item can be renamed without losing its effect, but it will lose its font color. This can be restored by reseting it's name at an anvil.
