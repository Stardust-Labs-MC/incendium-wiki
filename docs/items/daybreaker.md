# Description

Daybreaker is a hack and slash combo based sword, that allows for contant pressure on your foes and the ability to reposition yourself mid fight. While also dropping gold on mob kills.

The Daybreaker perform slashs and jabs in a 4 combo set, with the combo being 3 slashes into a jab before reseting. The combo moves foward when you swing the blade and it doesn't matter if you hit a foe or not, but if you don't do the next move in the combo fast enough it will reset. The slashes in the combo hit foes in a close area in front of you and and doesn't apply knockback to the hit mobs. While the jab has 2 effects, first is mobs hit by it will take a large amount of knockback, second is if you have above 3 ![Food](../assets/sprites/food_full.png){width="9"} the jab will also lunge the user foward similar to that of a lunge spear while at the cost of applying exhaustion.

- Foes slain by this sword have a chance to drop gold nuggets or ingots. Up to 3 items can be dropped at once
    * 95% for one gold nugget
    * 25% for one gold nugget
    * 5% for one gold ingot


This item has synergy with the Item [Prismatica](../items/prismatica.md), if there is a Prismatic Sword charge ready then whenever you lunge foward with the Daybreaker's jab then a sword will be summoned and will strike the area you lunged from.

This item is immune to burning in lava/explosions and most other means of destroying items

# Obtaining

The Daybreaker can be found in the basement of the [Sanctum](../structures/sanctum.md) at the end of a maze .

# History

The Daybreaker is a Patron item requested by ZombiePogman.


# Modifications
This item can be renamed without losing its effect, but it will lose its font color. This can be restored by reseting it's name at an anvil. It can also be grindstoned safely

The Daybreaker works with most sword enchantments except sweeping edge and can not be enchanted directly, you must combine enchant books in order to add any enchants
